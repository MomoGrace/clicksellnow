Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ClickSellNow PDF Tools handles information when you use this website. Contact: clicksellnow@proton.me.

Last updated: 2026-05-08

Information you provide

You may contact us by email and provide your email address, message content, and any details you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your question or feedback.

PDF and file processing

ClickSellNow tools are designed for browser-based processing where possible. This means the tool logic runs in your browser for common PDF tasks. We do not require you to create an account to use the public tools. You should still avoid using any online tool for documents that you are not allowed to process outside your own secure systems.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

This website may use cookies, analytics tools, and advertising services, including Google AdSense, to understand site performance, improve user experience, and display ads. Third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

Third-party libraries and services

Some browser tools may load trusted third-party JavaScript libraries from content delivery networks to perform PDF operations. These libraries help the browser read, create, or render PDF files.

How we use information

  • To operate and improve the website.
  • To respond to support emails.
  • To understand general site performance.
  • To maintain safety, prevent abuse, and comply with applicable requirements.

Data retention

Support emails may be retained as needed to respond to your inquiry and keep records of tool issues or feedback. You can request deletion of your support email conversation by contacting us.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for general document use and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us.

Contact

For privacy questions, email clicksellnow@proton.me.
