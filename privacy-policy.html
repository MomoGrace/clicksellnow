Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ClickSellNow PDF Tools handles information when you use this website and our tools.

Last updated: 2026-05-09
Contact: clicksellnow@proton.me

1. Information You Provide

You may contact us by email with feedback, questions, bug reports, or suggestions. When you do, you may provide:

  • Your email address
  • Message content
  • Any details you choose to include

We use this information to respond to your inquiry and keep records of support conversations. You may request deletion of your email conversation by contacting us.

2. PDF and File Processing

ClickSellNow tools are designed for browser-based processing where possible.

For supported tools and common PDF tasks, this means:

  • The tool logic runs in your browser
  • Your selected files are handled locally by your browser
  • You do not need to create an account to use public tools
  • Files are not intentionally stored on our servers for browser-based processing

However, you should understand:

  • Some browser features and third-party libraries may be loaded from trusted content delivery networks
  • Very large files may depend on your browser memory and device performance
  • You should avoid using online tools for documents you are not permitted to process outside your own secure systems
  • For highly sensitive documents, such as legal, medical, financial, or confidential work files, you should consider using approved internal tools or desktop software you control

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

How We Use Cookies

This website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Understand how visitors use the site
  • Measure website performance
  • Improve user experience
  • Support advertising services such as Google AdSense if enabled
  • Help prevent abuse and maintain security

Types of Cookies

Essential cookies may be used for basic website functionality, security, or session-related features.

Performance cookies may help us understand how visitors use the website so we can improve pages and tools.

Advertising cookies may be used by advertising networks such as Google to show ads and measure ad performance.

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block cookies, delete cookies, or receive alerts when cookies are being used. Blocking cookies may affect some website features.

4. Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand general website usage, such as:

  • How many people visit the website
  • Which pages are most popular
  • How long visitors spend on pages
  • What devices and browsers visitors use
  • What countries or regions visitors come from

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to collect usage information. This information helps us understand and improve the website.

Google Analytics data is processed by Google according to Google’s own privacy policies and data practices. You can manage your Google privacy settings at:

https://myaccount.google.com/privacy

You can also use Google’s Analytics opt-out tools where available:

https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout

5. Third-Party Advertising Services

This website may use Google AdSense or other advertising networks to display advertisements.

When advertising is enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites.

Specifically:

  • Google may use advertising cookies to serve ads based on visits to this site and other sites on the internet
  • Google and its partners may use cookies to personalize or measure ads
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings
  • Some cookies may still be used for frequency capping, ad reporting, fraud prevention, and security

You can manage personalized ads at:

https://adssettings.google.com

You may also disable cookies through your browser settings.

6. Third-Party Libraries and Services

Some ClickSellNow tools use third-party JavaScript libraries from content delivery networks to perform PDF operations in the browser.

These libraries may help your browser:

  • Read PDF file structures
  • Create or modify PDF files
  • Render PDF pages
  • Convert supported formats

These libraries are used to support browser-based functionality. They are loaded only when needed for specific pages or tools. We choose commonly used libraries and services, but users should still be careful when handling private or sensitive files online.

7. How We Use Information

We may use information you provide and general website usage information to:

  • Operate the website and tools
  • Respond to support emails and feedback
  • Improve website performance and usability
  • Understand which tools and guides are useful
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements
  • Improve guides and tools based on common user needs

8. Data Retention

Support emails may be retained as needed to respond to inquiries and maintain records of tool issues or feedback. You may request deletion by contacting us.

Analytics data is processed and stored by third-party analytics providers according to their own policies and retention settings.

Cookies may remain on your device for different periods depending on their purpose. You can delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access and misuse. However:

  • No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure
  • No storage system is completely risk-free
  • We cannot guarantee absolute security

For highly sensitive documents, use your own offline tools, approved internal systems, or secure software you control.

10. GDPR and International Privacy Laws

For users in the European Union, European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland, and other regions with privacy laws, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data.

Depending on your location, these rights may include:

  • The right to access personal data
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate data
  • The right to request deletion of personal data
  • The right to object to certain processing
  • The right to restrict certain processing
  • The right to withdraw consent where consent is required
  • The right to request information about how data is used

Where required by law, we aim to provide appropriate consent options for non-essential cookies and advertising technologies.

To exercise privacy rights, contact:

clicksellnow@proton.me

We will respond to reasonable requests in accordance with applicable law.

11. Children’s Privacy

ClickSellNow is not directed at children under 13 years old. The website is intended for general document work by students, office workers, teachers, professionals, small business owners, and everyday users.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at clicksellnow@proton.me so we can take appropriate steps.

12. External Links and Third-Party Services

ClickSellNow may link to external websites or load third-party services and libraries.

We are not responsible for:

  • Third-party website content
  • Third-party privacy practices
  • Third-party security practices
  • Third-party data handling

Please review the privacy policies of external websites and services before sharing information with them.

13. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect:

  • Changes in our data practices
  • Changes in laws or regulations
  • Improvements to our website or tools
  • Feedback from users

When we update this Privacy Policy, we may change the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of the website after changes means you accept the updated policy.

14. Your Privacy Choices

You have choices about your information.

For cookies:

  • Disable cookies in your browser settings
  • Clear cookies regularly
  • Use privacy-focused browsers or extensions

For analytics:

  • Use browser settings or extensions that limit tracking
  • Use Google Analytics opt-out tools where available

For advertising:

  • Manage personalized ads through Google Ads Settings
  • Disable cookies through your browser settings
  • Use privacy-focused tools if desired

For email:

  • You do not need to provide an email address to use basic tools
  • You only provide an email address if you choose to contact us

15. Contact Us

For questions, concerns, or requests related to this Privacy Policy, contact:

clicksellnow@proton.me

We take privacy questions seriously and will respond when we can.

16. Transparency Summary

ClickSellNow is designed to be practical and transparent.

  • Supported tools use browser-based processing where possible
  • Public tools do not require account creation
  • Contact by email is optional
  • Cookies and analytics may be used to improve the site
  • Advertising cookies may be used if Google AdSense or other advertising services are enabled
  • Users can manage cookies and personalized ads through browser and Google settings
  • Sensitive documents should be handled carefully and may require offline or approved internal tools

For more information about using this website, please also review our Terms of Use.
